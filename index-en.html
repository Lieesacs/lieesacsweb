About Liée Sacs

Liée Sacs was born from the desire to create unique bags combining elegance, delicacy and comfort. Each piece is carefully designed and handmade in Barcelona, using carefully selected materials to guarantee quality, durability and style. Our crochet bags are 100% handmade and made to order. The creation process takes between 1 and 2 weeks.
